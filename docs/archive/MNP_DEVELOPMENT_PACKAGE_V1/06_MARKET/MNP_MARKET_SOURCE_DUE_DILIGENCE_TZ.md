# MNP MARKET SOURCE DUE DILIGENCE - TZ

Before production ingestion of Work.ua, robota.ua, DSZ or other sources, document:
- official API/data export/partner options;
- terms and permitted reuse;
- robots/scraping restrictions;
- attribution;
- caching/storage rights;
- rate limits;
- salary/vacancy definitions;
- historical availability;
- geography;
- update cadence;
- commercial terms;
- contact/partnership route.

No scraper is a product requirement until legal/technical access is approved.
