# MNP PRODUCT CONCEPT NOTE V1

## Problem
Adults who already have education, experience and skills often do not need another generic personality test. They need to know which real careers they can enter now, which adjacent transitions reuse their capital, where income can grow, what gaps matter, and what the Ukrainian market currently offers.

## Solution
MNP is a Personal Career Navigation System:
`Career Card → Career Universe → Feasibility → Matching → Market → Gap → Route → Opportunity`.

## Primary users
- adults seeking work;
- unemployed / people who lost work;
- IDPs;
- veterans returning to civilian careers;
- people considering a career change;
- Ukrainians abroad evaluating a return to Ukraine;
- professionals seeking higher income or better use of existing skills.

## Core value
Not “discover your archetype”, but:
- what you already have;
- what careers are realistic;
- what you can start now;
- what transition is fastest;
- what may pay more;
- what to learn/prove/reframe;
- what real next action is available.

## V1 success
A user can create a Career Card, receive explainable recommendations across 50 careers, open a career compatibility view, understand gaps and feasibility, and receive an actionable route.
